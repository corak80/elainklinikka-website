# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Pet owners (dogs, cats, small mammals, some exotics) in Vaasa and the Ostrobothnian coast, Finnish- and Swedish-speaking, with a smaller English-speaking group. ~96% of ad traffic and most site visits are on mobile. Typical situations: a new puppy/kitten, a vaccination or check-up, a worrying symptom, or a procedure recommended by another vet. A secondary audience is referring vets in the region.

## Product Purpose

The public website of Eläinklinikka Saari / Djurklinik Saari, a private small-animal clinic at Gerbyntie 18, 65230 Vaasa (district Vetokannas / Dragnäsbäck, at the "pukinkulma" / Bockska hörnet bend). It exists so that an owner can understand what the clinic does, trust it, and book (online via my.provet.com or by phone 06 321 7300). Success = bookings and calls from people in the catchment, and owners who arrive prepared.

## Positioning

Independent and family-owned since 1989 (not part of a chain; the vet who examines the pet makes the decisions), with six vets and eight veterinary nurses and in-house equipment a one-vet practice rarely has: dental care under general anaesthesia with dental X-ray, digital X-ray, abdominal and cardiac ultrasound, endoscopy (gastro/oto/rhino/cysto/bronchoscopy), orthopaedic surgery (TTA, lateral suture), an in-house laboratory, and an ISFM Cat Friendly Clinic Silver accreditation (first in Ostrobothnia, 2020). Also a known wildlife / hedgehog rehabilitation partner (Siilisaari ry).

## Operating Context

Open Mon–Fri 7:45–17:00; no own out-of-hours emergency service (regional on-call numbers are given instead). Drop-in vaccinations Tuesdays 15:30–16:00. Insurance direct billing with LähiTapiola (SV: LokalTapiola), Agria and Pohjola. Free parking at the door; bus stops on Gerbyntie. Prices are published on /hinnasto/ and kept in `pricing.md`. Service pages exist in FI (`/palvelut/...`), SV (`/sv/tjanster/...`) and EN (`/en/services/...`); health articles in all three languages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site (GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare), hand-written HTML + `css/style.css` + `js/main.js`; article/service pages generated by `build-articles.js` (currently drifted — edit outputs and generator by hand), homepages by `build-homepages.js`, sitemap lastmod by `build-sitemap.js` (URL membership is manual).
- Three languages with hreflang pairs; FI is x-default. Finland-Swedish, never Sweden-Swedish.
- Never use "specialist" / "erikoiseläinlääkäri" / "erikoisala" (protected title). No invented memberships, accreditations or licence numbers. No 24/7 or emergency-clinic claims. Any address, Y-tunnus, phone, opening hours or price on a page must come from the repo (`pricing.md`, existing pages), never from memory.
- Location wording: FI Vetokannas / pukinkulma, SV Dragnäsbäck / Bockska hörnet. "Bockis" is retired. "Dragnäsbäckintie" does not exist.
- Founding year 1989 (never 2003). Vet count six (since Aug 2026).
- Conversion tracking: tel: links call `gtag_report_conversion()`, booking buttons fire `fbq('track','Schedule')`; keep both on any new CTA.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Eläinklinikka Saari" (SV "Djurklinik Saari"). Logo `images/logo.png`; Cat Friendly Clinic Silver header badge `images/cat-friendly-badge-header.webp`. Primary colour #E58DB4 (pink) with dark text #1a1a2e, Inter as the only typeface (self-hosted). Voice: warm, plain, factual, addresses the reader as sinä / du; owner vocabulary, not clinical jargon. Site chrome (header, nav, footer, CTA box) is shared by every page.

## Evidence on Hand

- Google rating 4.6/5 (site aggregateRating, verified 06-10-2026), 281 reviews (as of 18-07-2026); review page `/arvostelut/`.
- Press: Yle (2020, first Cat Friendly Clinic in Ostrobothnia) and Vasabladet articles, linked from the cat page.
- Vets' training that may be named: Leena Sandström — ESAVS Certificate in Cardiology, Finnish Kennel Club official heart examiner; Pamela Kvarngård — AOVET and ESAVS orthopaedic surgery programmes, Accesia Academy dental; Assaf Wydra, Nina Haglund, Merja Autio — Accesia Academy dental; Nina — ESAVS soft-tissue surgery.
- Photos in `images/` and `media/` (clinic, team, equipment); no stock imagery policy stated.
- Absent, do not fabricate: testimonials/quotes, case counts, success rates, benchmarks, memberships.

## Product Principles

1. Honest first: say plainly what the clinic does not do (no night emergencies, no 24/7) before what it does.
2. Facts over adjectives: named equipment, named training, published prices.
3. One action always visible: call or book online, on every page, on mobile first.
4. Bilingual by default: FI and SV are equals, not a translation afterthought.
5. Nothing on a page that the repo cannot prove.
