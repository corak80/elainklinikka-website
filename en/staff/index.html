Staff

Saari Animal Clinic's care team consists of 13 skilled professionals — experienced veterinarians and veterinary technicians. Our veterinarians have decades of experience in small-animal medicine and further training in areas including orthopedics, cardiology, dentistry and endoscopy. Our technicians care for patients warmly before, during and after procedures. Meet our team below.

Veterinarians

Leena Sandström

Veterinarian

I qualified as a veterinarian in 1997 and have worked as a small-animal vet for almost my entire career.

I have specialized in heart disease and completed the European ESAVS Certificate in Small Animal Veterinary Practice / Cardiology. I am authorized to perform the Finnish Kennel Club's official cardiac examinations (auscultation, ultrasound and ECG). I have trained in abdominal ultrasound on Finnish courses (Fennovet) and in Europe (ESAVS).

My professional interests focus on heart disease and internal medicine in dogs and cats.

Through careful examination and up-to-date knowledge, I aim to ensure that your pet receives appropriate care.

Areas of expertise: Heart disease, internal medicine, cardiac and abdominal ultrasound

Pamela Kvarngård

Veterinarian

I have worked as a small-animal veterinarian since 2013, and today my work focuses especially on orthopedic patients. I have pursued further training in areas including orthopedic surgery (AOVET, ESAVS, etc.) and dental disease (Accesia Academy).

It is important to me to stay up to date with new research, treatment methods and techniques — continuous training is an essential part of providing the best possible care.

My core professional interests are orthopedics, soft-tissue surgery and dental care. I find it especially rewarding to help animals regain their comfort, mobility and a good quality of life, and this motivates me in my work every day.

Areas of expertise: Orthopedics, soft-tissue surgery, dental care

Assaf Wydra

Veterinarian

I have worked as a small-animal veterinarian since 2011, and today my work focuses mainly on dental care, endoscopy and the care of wild animals. I have completed further training in veterinary dentistry (Accesia Academy) as well as in endoscopy and wildlife care.

Following and making use of new technology is an essential part of my professional development and helps me provide my patients with the highest possible standard of care.

My professional interests are especially dental care, endoscopy and the practical application of new technological innovations in clinical work. Alongside clinic work, I actively do voluntary pro bono work with wild animals, which I find a very meaningful and important part of my role as a veterinarian.

Areas of expertise: Dental care, endoscopy, wildlife care, technological innovation

Nina Haglund

Veterinarian

I have worked as a veterinarian since 2016/2017 and have been part of the Saari Animal Clinic team since 2020. It is important to me to combine a calm and empathetic approach with a way of working that emphasizes patient safety and strong clinical skill — I want both the animals and their owners to feel safe at my appointments.

Before my veterinary career I qualified as a pharmacist, which gives a strong foundation for understanding pharmacology and medication. I have complemented my skills with further training in dental disease (Accesia Academy) and studies in soft-tissue surgery (ESAVS).

My professional interests focus especially on surgery, dental care and dermatology. In my work I am motivated by continuous development — and above all by being able to support animal welfare every day.

Areas of expertise: Surgery, dental care, dermatology

Merja Autio

Veterinarian

I graduated in the summer of 2025 from Eesti Maaülikool in Tartu and have worked as a veterinarian since 2024. I have been part of the Saari Animal Clinic team since April 2025. My particular interests are soft-tissue surgery and exotic animals, and my goal is to train further in these areas in the future.

A calm and individual approach, together with reliable, evidence-based care of the animal, is important to me. I find my work meaningful and appreciate its variety and professional challenges.

Areas of expertise: Soft-tissue surgery, exotic animals

Emilia Svahn

Veterinary Technician

For several years I have studied and worked with animals, which has long been my passion. In 2023 I qualified as an animal caretaker, which gave me a strong foundation in animal welfare and care. Even then I knew I wanted to deepen my skills, and in 2024 I trained as a veterinary technician.

I also very much enjoy tasks related to dental care. It is rewarding to be able to promote animals' long-term health and quality of life through good oral care.

Working with animals and being part of their care brings me great joy and pride. Every day gives me the chance to grow, learn more and continue doing valuable work for animals and their owners.

Interests: Anesthesia, surgical procedures and dental care
