Saari in the news

Saari Animal Clinic and our veterinarians have been featured in several Finnish media outlets. Below is a collection of articles where our clinic or our veterinarians have been mentioned — most recent first. Articles are in Finnish or Swedish; translations are available via Google Translate.

2026

28-10-2026 · Yle Österbotten (in Swedish)
Veterinärens råd: Borsta djurets tänder för att undvika dyra veterinärbesök
Our veterinarian Pamela Kvarngård on the cost of vet care: when money is tight, care can start with examining or treating the most common cause, and insurance, a savings account and daily tooth brushing help keep costs down.

28-08-2026 · Österbottens Tidning (in Swedish)
Veterinärens råd när raketerna drar i gång
Vets and pharmacies see more demand for calming medicine as the end-of-summer fireworks approach. Our veterinarian Pamela Kvarngård says many owners know that help is available for dogs afraid of fireworks.
The same article in Vasabladet: ”Onödigt att hunden ska lida”

22-07-2026 · Vasabladet (in Swedish)
Här rehabiliteras igelkottar
Vasabladet visited the clinic: Johanna Tidström, together with volunteers and veterinarian Assaf Wydra, runs a rehabilitation centre for hedgehogs in need on the clinic's upper floor.

21-06-2026 · Vasabladet (in Swedish)
Tre frågor till veterinären om fästingmedel
Our veterinarian Pamela Kvarngård answers three questions about tick treatments and how they affect pets and the environment around them.

20-06-2026 · Yle Österbotten (in Swedish)
Skadade igelkottar får hjälp i Vasa
Yle's video about the new hedgehog help centre in Vaasa: volunteers and veterinarians care for injured hedgehogs, which are released back into nature once they have recovered.

17-06-2026 · Yle Vega Österbotten (radio, in Swedish)
Igelkottarna rehabiliteras vid nytt center i Vasa
A 12-minute radio interview: reporter Kati Enkvist talks with Johanna Tidström and our veterinarian Assaf Wydra about the new hedgehog rehabilitation centre in Vaasa.

02-06-2026 · Yle Österbotten (in Swedish)
Rekordmånga fästingar men få husdjur blir allvarligt sjuka
Despite a record number of tick sightings, few pets become seriously ill. Our veterinarian Pamela Kvarngård says tablets and collars protect well and recommends them over natural remedies.

24-04-2026 · MTV Uutiset (in Finnish)
Luna-koiran sydän petti yllättäen — eläinlääkäriltä ruokavaliovaroitus
Six-year-old Luna's DCM heart disease was linked to a grain-free diet. Our veterinarian Assaf Wydra warns against grain-free feeding without medical justification.

10-01-2026 · Iltalehti (in Finnish)
Suomea kiertää pakettiauto, josta varoitetaan vakavasti
According to our clinic, dogs have been given questionable dental treatments in a van at a petrol station. Tartar must also be removed from the gum pockets, which cannot be done while the pet is awake.
