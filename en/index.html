Our Services

We offer comprehensive veterinary services for your pet

🚨

Emergency

Daily reserved acute slots weekdays 7:45–17. Evenings and weekends: regional hotline 0600 399 299. First aid guidance for emergencies.
🪡

Acupuncture

Acupuncture is used for pain relief and for the treatment of some diseases. Administered by Assaf, who has completed further training in canine and equine acupuncture.
🩸

Blood Tests

Most samples are examined in our own laboratory, so you can get results during the same visit.
🔬

Dermatology & Allergies

Skin diseases are one of the most common reasons to seek veterinary care. About one in ten dogs in Finland has allergies, which often cause skin and ear infections. Typical symptoms include paw licking, ear scratching, and face rubbing. When allergy is suspected, we first try to rule out food as a cause, and if necessary, collect allergy blood samples.
🤰

Pregnancy Diagnosis

Ultrasound can confirm pregnancy from approximately day 24. X-ray examination can provide a more accurate estimate of the number of puppies from approximately day 50.
📷

Endoscopy

We perform video-otoscopy (ear canal examination and flushing), rhinoscopy (nasal cavity examination), gastroscopy (gastrointestinal examination), cystoscopy (urinary tract examination) and bronchoscopy (airway examination).
🍖

Therapeutic Diets

We carry special therapeutic diets used as part of the treatment for various conditions. Recommended for osteoarthritis, renal failure, gastrointestinal diseases, food allergies, urinary tract diseases, and weight management. Brands: Royal Canin, Specific, Hill's.
💪

Wellness & Rehabilitation

Wellness and rehabilitation services to assess and improve your pet's mobility and function. Common reasons include reduced mobility, pain following trauma, and post-surgical recovery. Services: gait analysis, deep massage, electrotherapy, customized training programs.
🩺

Health Examinations

During health checks we can detect changes that may cause health problems in the long run. The vet performs a full clinical examination, listens to the heart and lungs and, if necessary, takes blood samples.
❤️

Heart Diseases

Heart diseases are common, especially in certain breeds. Early treatment can extend your pet's lifespan and improve quality of life. We offer cardiac ultrasound, ECG and Holter monitoring. We also perform official heart ultrasound examinations for dogs. We are authorized to perform official heart auscultation examinations.
✂️

Castration

We castrate cats, dogs and rabbits. Dog castration can prevent prostate problems and urine marking. Chemical castration with a hormonal implant is also available (6 or 12-month duration).
🔪

Soft Tissue Surgery

Spays, neuters, caesarean sections, tumor removals, foreign body extraction, bladder stone surgery, other abdominal surgeries, and eye and ear procedures.
🦴

Orthopedic Surgery

The clinic has a dedicated orthopedic surgery room with a higher level of sterility. We have been performing knee surgeries since 2017. Cruciate ligament repair with two methods: lateral suture technique stabilizes the joint with synthetic support material, and TTA (tibial tuberosity advancement) alters knee biomechanics by advancing the tibial tuberosity forward. Also patellar luxation surgery, fracture repairs, amputations and femoral head ostectomy for chronic luxation or Legg-Perthes disease.
🧪

Laboratory

Well-equipped in-house laboratory: blood samples, urine samples, blood smears, skin and ear samples. Most results available during your visit. Analyses: hematology, clinical chemistry, electrolytes, hormones, urine cultures, cytology.
📋

Official Examinations

Official hip, elbow and back X-rays. Official knee examinations. Official heart auscultations. Official heart ultrasound examinations for dogs.
☢️

X-ray

Digital X-ray. Recommended for limping, respiratory or cardiac symptoms, acute vomiting, urinary problems, tumor suspicion, and official screening X-rays.
🕊️

Final Farewell – Euthanasia

We strive to make the final farewell peaceful and unhurried. Your pet first receives a sedative with pain relief, followed by an anesthetic. Options: home burial, cremation, or individual cremation with urn.
🏥

Sterilization

A common procedure for dogs, cats and rabbits. Sterilization helps prevent hormonal diseases including mammary tumors and uterine infections. General anesthesia with comprehensive pain management.
🦷

Dental Care

Dental disorders are very common — by age 3, most dogs and cats have some dental inflammation. Services: tartar removal with ultrasound, dental X-rays, surgical tooth extractions, deciduous teeth removals, and removal of unerupted teeth and cysts. All procedures under general anesthesia with inhalation anesthesia, intravenous fluids and comprehensive pain management.
📡

Ultrasound

High-quality ultrasound: abdominal examinations, uterine examinations, liver, kidney and other organ examinations, and heart ultrasound.
💉

Vaccinations

Vaccinations are an important part of preventive healthcare. Puppies are usually vaccinated at 12 and 16 weeks of age, kittens likewise. Adults are vaccinated every 1–3 years.
🐕

Puppy Examinations

The veterinarian examines the puppy thoroughly from head to toe and issues a health certificate. A microchip can be implanted during the visit.
💊

Internal Medicine

Internal medicine covers a wide range of animal diseases: diabetes, Cushing's disease, pancreatitis, anaemia, autoimmune diseases. Thorough diagnostics and treatment planning.
👁️

Ophthalmology

Eye diseases are common in pets. Corneal ulcers, glaucoma, dry eye, cataracts, eyelid diseases. Examination and treatment during the same visit.
🛂

EU Pet Passport

EU pet passports, rabies vaccination, titre testing and health certificates. All travel documents under one roof.
😴

Anesthesia

Anesthesia is close to our hearts. We continuously update our education, refresh our protocols and monitor patients closely throughout every procedure. The clinic has advanced monitoring equipment, four anesthesia machines and two ventilators to ensure safe anesthesia.
🧼

Hygiene

A high level of hygiene is of utmost importance to us. Examination tables are disinfected between patients and blankets and pads are washed after every use. We use UV light to disinfect clinic areas and filters with inhalation anesthesia. Floors are disinfected twice daily.

We are a Silver accredited Cat Friendly Clinic

We want to minimise stress for cats visiting our clinic, so we are an International Cat Care Cat Friendly Clinic.

In 2020 we became the first Cat Friendly Clinic in Ostrobothnia, as reported by Yle (in Finnish) and Vasabladet (in Swedish).

THIS IS OUR PROMISE TO YOU:

  • We are gentle and caring with cats
  • We understand the needs of cats
  • We have cat friendly facilities

Frequently asked questions

Where is Eläinklinikka Saari located?

We are at Gerbyntie 18, 65230 Vaasa, in the Dragnäsbäck district at Pukinkulma. We are easy to reach from the surrounding municipalities too.

What are Eläinklinikka Saari's opening hours?

The clinic is open Monday to Friday 7:45–17 and closed at weekends. We reserve acute slots every day for urgent cases.

Do you have emergency service in the evenings and at weekends?

In the evenings and at weekends, call the Northern emergency-area number 0600 399 299. On weekdays 7:45–17 we have acute slots available daily.

Which animals do you treat?

We treat dogs, cats and other small pets such as rabbits and rodents. We are an officially Cat Friendly certified clinic.

How do I book an appointment with the vet?

You can book online or by calling 06 321 7300. No referral is needed.

What languages do you serve in?

We serve in Finnish, Swedish and English.
