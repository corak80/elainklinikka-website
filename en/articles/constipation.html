Constipation in Dogs and Cats — Signs, Home Care and When to Call

Veterinarian Assaf Wydra, Eläinklinikka Saari

Most dogs poo one to three times a day and most cats once or twice. Straining, small hard poo or no poo for two days can mean constipation. Mild cases often settle at home, but straining can mean something serious.

Call at Once

Call us on (06) 321 7300 at once if a male cat strains in the litter tray, even if it looks like constipation: his bladder may be blocked, which can kill him within a day or two. Also call at once if your pet tries to pee but nothing comes out, strains and vomits, has a swollen or painful belly, may have swallowed a bone or an object, is very weak, or has a red lump or tube sticking out of its bottom. Outside opening hours, call the regional emergency vet on 0600 399 299.

Call the Same Day

Call us the same day if your pet has not pooed for two days (a kitten: one day), keeps straining without result, has blood in its poo or is off its food or dull, and for any constipated puppy or kitten. Straining can also be a bladder problem or diarrhoea, and a badly constipated pet may pass only a little runny poo.

Common Causes

Common causes are too little water, eating bones or hair, a dirty litter tray, stress and arthritis pain. Older or overweight cats and cats with kidney disease are more at risk. In male dogs, especially uncastrated ones, an enlarged prostate or a hernia beside the bottom can be the cause.

Home Care

If your adult pet is otherwise well, help it get more water: wet food, several water bowls and, for some cats, a fountain. Keep the litter tray clean and brush long-haired cats. Walks help dogs; don’t give them bones. A high-fibre diet helps many pets but not all, so ask us first; we sell these diets. Don’t give any laxative, oil or enema without asking us: laxatives and some enemas made for people can be dangerous, especially for cats. Oil syringed into the mouth, even cooking oil, can go into the lungs. Call us if it gets worse or two days pass without a poo.

At the Clinic

We can take X-rays and blood tests (results during the visit), empty the bowel with an enema under sedation and give a drip in day care. A pet that is often constipated may need a special diet or medicine long-term. Book online (Akuutti ja sairas lemmikki → Sairaskäynti, muu vaiva) or call us.

Contact Us

Unsure? Call us on (06) 321 7300 or email info@saarivet.fi with photos. If it is urgent, call — don’t email.
