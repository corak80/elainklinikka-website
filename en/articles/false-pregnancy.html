False Pregnancy in Dogs — Signs, Home Care and When to Call

Veterinarian Assaf Wydra, Eläinklinikka Saari

False pregnancy is common in unspayed bitches. After a heat, a bitch’s hormones change as they would in a real pregnancy, whether or not she was mated. In some bitches this brings on signs, usually six to eight weeks after the heat. It is a natural hormonal state, not an illness, and the signs usually pass by themselves within one to three weeks.

Signs

Nesting, carrying toys around and guarding them like puppies, restlessness, whining, being less active, eating a little less, and swollen teats that may leak milk or a watery fluid. Some bitches become clingy; others become irritable and may growl or snap near their “puppies” — keep children away. If there is any chance she was mated, she may really be pregnant: book a pregnancy scan soon — it tells the two apart.

What Helps at Home

Keep her busy with walks and play, and while she is out on a walk, put away the toys she mothers. Don’t rub, squeeze or milk the teats: it makes them produce more milk. If she licks them, a soft body suit or a cone can stop it. Don’t limit her water unless a vet tells you to. Most bitches need nothing more.

When to Call

Call at once if, in the weeks or months after her heat, she drinks a lot, vomits, is listless, refuses food or has a discharge from the vulva: this can be pyometra, a dangerous infection of the womb. A discharge isn’t always seen, so if she seems ill, don’t assume it is just a false pregnancy. If the area around a teat is hot, red, painful or clearly harder than the rest, or the milk looks bloody or like pus, she may have an infected mammary gland (mastitis): call us the same day, and at once if that area turns dark or she has a fever or seems very unwell. Outside opening hours, call the regional emergency vet on 0600 399 299. Book a visit if the signs last more than about three weeks or are hard for her or you to live with: a vet can give medicine that usually dries up the milk and settles the signs.

Will It Happen Again?

A bitch who has had one false pregnancy often has another after later heats. Spaying is the only lasting way to prevent them. We spay about three months after her heat has ended, and only once the false pregnancy is completely over: spaying too soon can start a false pregnancy or keep one going, sometimes for months. We go through the benefits and drawbacks of spaying with you.
