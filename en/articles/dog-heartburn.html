Heartburn in Dogs — Signs, What Helps and When to Call

Veterinarian Assaf Wydra, Eläinklinikka Saari

Dogs can’t tell us if they have heartburn. Its signs are commonly blamed on stomach acid flowing back into the gullet, but research suggests this often isn’t the cause: many such dogs have a stomach or gut problem that a diet change can help. Signs that keep coming back need a vet.

Signs

Licking the lips, swallowing or gulping again and again, drooling, licking floors or furniture, burping, restlessness and eating less. Some dogs bring up yellow foam on an empty stomach, often early in the morning. Eating grass on its own is rarely a sign of illness.

Call at Once

Call us on (06) 321 7300 at once if your dog retches again and again but brings up nothing or only a little foam, especially if it is restless or drooling or its belly looks swollen; may have swallowed an object; has blood in its vomit or black, tar-like stools; or struggles to swallow or breathe, or is very weak. Outside opening hours, call the regional emergency vet on 0600 399 299.

What Helps at Home

Feed smaller meals more often; if your dog brings up yellow foam in the morning but is otherwise well, a small late-evening meal may help. Avoid fatty food and table scraps. No research shows cottage cheese helps, and dairy upsets some dogs’ stomachs. We sell easily digestible diets. Don’t give human medicines without asking us: some harm dogs.

When to Book a Visit

Book a visit if signs last more than a few days or keep coming back, or your dog vomits often, loses weight or brings food back up without effort. Call the same day if your dog coughs after eating, or if signs start after an anaesthetic or a new medicine; if it is pain relief, give no more until you have spoken to us.

At the Clinic

When needed, we do blood tests (results during the visit), X-rays, ultrasound or a gastroscopy under general anaesthesia to look at the gullet and stomach and take samples. Treatment depends on the cause and may include a diet change or medicine to reduce stomach acid. Book online (Akuutti ja sairas lemmikki → Sairaskäynti, muu vaiva) or call us.

Contact Us

Unsure? Call us on (06) 321 7300 or email info@saarivet.fi with photos or a video. If it is urgent, call — don’t email.
