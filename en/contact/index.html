Directions

By car: The clinic is in the Dragnäsbäck district, at the junction of Gerbyntie and Dragnäsbäckintie (the Bockis bend). It's about a 5-minute drive from Vaasa city centre. Drive south along Wolffintie and turn onto Gerbyntie — the clinic is on the right. Free parking is available in the car park in front of the clinic, with space for several cars.

By bus: The nearest bus stop is on Dragnäsbäckintie, about a 200-metre walk from the clinic. Vaasa local bus line 1 runs along the route. Check timetables at vaasa.fi.

Phone and email

Phone: (06) 321 7300
Email: info@saarivet.fi

We answer the phone on weekdays during opening hours. For non-urgent matters you can also send an email or a WhatsApp message, and we'll reply as soon as possible.

Opening hours

Mon–Fri: 7:45–17:00
Sat–Sun: Closed

The clinic operates by appointment. We recommend booking in advance so we can reserve enough time for your pet's care. In urgent situations, call (06) 321 7300 and we'll direct you to the right on-call clinic.

Booking an appointment

You can book an appointment in two ways:

By phone: Call (06) 321 7300 on weekdays 7:45–17. Our reception staff will help you find a suitable time.

Online booking: Book an appointment around the clock in our online booking system at my.provet.com/elainklinikka-saari. Online booking is fast and easy to use.

What to bring to your appointment

Please bring any previous medical records or discharge summaries, current medication and your pet's vaccination card to the appointment. If your pet is insured, bring the insurance certificate — we offer direct billing with LähiTapiola, Agria and Pohjola. Cats should be brought in a carrier and dogs on a lead.

Emergencies

Our clinic does not provide round-the-clock emergency care. In emergencies outside opening hours, call the clinic on (06) 321 7300, where a recorded message will direct you to the nearest on-call clinic. In acute, life-threatening situations, contact an on-call veterinarian immediately.
